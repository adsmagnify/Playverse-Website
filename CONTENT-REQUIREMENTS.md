# Asaiverse Website — Content Requirements

This document lists everything that should be prepared and added to the website. It does not include current placeholder copy or technical implementation details.

---

## 1. Brand & Identity

- One-line tagline or value proposition
- Short brand description (1–2 sentences for footer and SEO)
- Longer brand story (who you are, why you exist, what makes you different)
- Brand voice guidelines (tone: professional, hype, casual, etc.)
- Logo (primary and optional light/dark variants)
- Favicon and app icon
- Social sharing preview image (for link previews on WhatsApp, Twitter, etc.)

---



## 2. Contact & Business Information

- Primary contact email
- Phone number (with country code)
- Optional secondary contact (partnerships, press, support)
- Physical office or studio address (if you want it public)
- Business hours or response-time expectation
- Working contact form destination (where inquiries should go)
- Confirmation message users should see after submitting the form

---



## 3. Social & Community Links

- Instagram
- YouTube
- Discord
- X (Twitter)
- Any other platforms you use (Twitch, LinkedIn, WhatsApp community, etc.)

---



## 4. Site-Wide Messaging

- Browser tab title
- Search engine description (meta description)
- Primary call-to-action text (e.g. “Book an event”, “View events”, “Get in touch”)
- Secondary call-to-action text
- Navigation labels (confirm Home, Events, About, Contact or any changes)

---



## 5. Homepage Content



### Hero section

- Main headline
- Supporting subheadline
- Short paragraph explaining what Asaiverse does
- Primary button label and destination
- Secondary button label and destination
- Optional scroll prompt or micro-copy at the bottom of the hero



### Featured scroll moment (hero animation reveal)

- Eyebrow label (e.g. “Next event”, “Featured”, “Now live”)
- Featured event or campaign name
- Game or category
- Location or region
- Short description (1–2 lines)
- Link destination (event page, registration, etc.)



### Stats / credibility section

- Section heading
- Supporting line of copy
- Up to four metrics with labels (e.g. events hosted, cities, audience reach, prize pools)
- Decide whether numbers must be exact or approximate



### Game categories / disciplines

- Section title
- List of game types or formats you actually operate in
- For each category:
  - Title
  - Tags or example titles
  - Short description
  - Supporting visual



### Upcoming / featured events block

- Section title
- Live or schedule ticker content (if used)
- Which events to highlight on the homepage
- “View all” button label



### Playbook / principles section

- Section title
- Intro paragraph
- Four (or more) principle cards, each with:
  - Title
  - Short explanation



### FAQ section

- Section title
- Five to eight question-and-answer pairs covering:
  - What events you run
  - Games and formats supported
  - Production and broadcast capabilities
  - Minimum event size or scope
  - How to book or partner with you
  - Timelines, pricing approach, or regions served (as applicable)



### Final call-to-action section

- Headline
- Supporting copy
- Optional stat or proof point
- Button label and destination



### Scrolling marquee (optional)

- List of games, formats, or phrases to rotate in the ticker

---



## 6. Events Page Content

- Page hero badge or label
- Page headline
- Short page description
- Full list of events to display



### For each event

- Event name
- URL-friendly slug
- Game or category
- City / venue / region
- Season, year, or date range
- One-line summary (for cards)
- Full description (for detail page)
- Whether it should be featured on the homepage
- Hero or card image
- Optional gallery images
- Registration or ticket link (if live)
- Prize pool, format, team count, schedule (optional detail fields)
- Status: upcoming, live, completed, or on demand

---



## 7. Event Detail Page Content

Per event (in addition to the list above):

- Long-form briefing copy
- Key facts sidebar: category, location, season/dates
- Call-to-action (e.g. “Register”, “Host this format”, “Contact us”)
- Related or recommended events to show at the bottom

---



## 8. About Page Content

- Page hero badge or label
- Page headline
- Hero description paragraph
- Origin story (how Asaiverse started)
- What you do today (services, regions, types of events)
- Optional milestones or timeline
- Stats block (can mirror homepage or be About-specific)
- Principles / creed section (can mirror homepage or be unique)
- Call-to-action to contact or view events

## 9. Contact Page Content

- Page hero badge or label
- Page headline
- Hero description (what happens after someone reaches out)
- Form section title
- Form fields to collect (e.g. name, org, email, game title, city, budget, message)
- Field labels and placeholder hints
- Submit button label
- Success message after submission
- Displayed email and phone on the page

## 13. Optional Sections (More to add in future)

- Team / founders bios and photos
- Partners and sponsors (logos + names)
- Past events archive or gallery
- Testimonials from players, orgs, or brands
- Press mentions or media kit
- Careers or hiring page
- Blog or news / announcements
- Privacy policy and terms of use
- FAQ additions for ticketing, refunds, or eligibility rules

---



## 14. Content to Decide Internally

Before writing copy, align on:

- Target audience (players, orgs, brands, campuses, venues)
- Geographic focus (cities, India-only, global)
- Which games and formats are core vs occasional
- Which events are real vs planned vs past
- Whether stats should be published or kept vague early on
- Tone balance between esports hype and professional credibility
- What the primary conversion goal is (inquiries, registrations, followers)

---



## 15. Recommended Handoff Format

When gathering content, prepare one section per area above. For events, use a consistent template for every entry so nothing is missed. For images, supply high-resolution originals; the site will handle sizing.

Once this content is ready, it can be added to the website in a single content update pass.
